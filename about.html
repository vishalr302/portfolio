About

I work where data, software, and practical problem-solving meet.

I am pursuing a B.S. in Statistics and Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University. My experience includes nonprofit data analysis, software development, LLM-supported education research, NLP, and computer vision.

Background

What I have worked on

At EcoServants, I built data tools for two different research problems: identifying potential nonprofit funders from large IRS datasets and organizing evidence about wildfire smoke events affecting the Northeast.

At Bighelp for Education, I worked on a nonprofit website with PHP, HTML/CSS, Python, and SQL. I also reviewed, tested, and debugged AI-generated code changes as part of a four-developer team.

As a research assistant at Carnegie Mellon, I helped evaluate LLM-powered Socratic Books for a group of 30 students and translated feedback into more than eight recommendations for improving the learning experience.

Approach

How I work

  • I break open-ended problems into readable pipelines with clear inputs, transformations, and outputs.
  • I prefer explainable methods when a user needs to understand why a result was produced.
  • I include validation, tests, and explicit limitations so a prototype is honest about what it can and cannot do.
  • I am comfortable working with mentors and teammates, reviewing code, tracking progress, and turning feedback into revisions.
